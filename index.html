Diduno

Founder / builder · Oct 2026 – present · diduno.org

Student product aimed at Temple first. Puts cheap or free .edu perks in one place, plus a campus map, live finds, events, a forum, and Microsoft school sign-in. I write the product and the code. About 50 of my commits are in the app.

  • ~335 digital student perks with eligibility, sources, and last-checked dates. GitHub Actions loads them into Postgres daily.
